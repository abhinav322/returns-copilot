# EU Returns Policy

## Standard Return Window

Customers in supported European Union markets may return eligible products within 30 days of delivery.

Returned items must be unused, unworn and in their original condition.

A valid order number must be provided.

## Final Sale Items

Items clearly marked as final sale are generally not eligible for voluntary returns.

Consumer rights relating to defective or incorrectly supplied products remain unaffected.

## Damaged Products

Damaged products should be reported to customer support as soon as possible.

The customer may be asked to provide photographs showing the damage.

Support staff will review the case before deciding the appropriate resolution.

## Refund Processing

Approved refunds are issued to the original payment method.

Processing time may vary by payment provider and country.
