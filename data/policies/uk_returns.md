# UK Returns Policy

## Standard Return Window

Customers in the United Kingdom may return eligible items within 30 days of delivery.

Returned products must be unused, unworn and in their original condition.

Proof of purchase or a valid order number is required.

## Final Sale Items

Products marked as final sale cannot normally be returned or exchanged.

The final sale restriction does not remove statutory rights where an item is faulty or incorrectly supplied.

## Damaged or Faulty Items

Customers who receive a damaged or faulty product should contact customer support.

Faulty products may be reviewed separately from the standard 30 day return policy.

The support team may request photographs or other evidence of the fault.

## Refund Processing

Approved refunds are returned to the original payment method.

Refund processing normally begins after the returned product has been inspected.

Customers should allow additional time for their bank or payment provider to process the refund.
