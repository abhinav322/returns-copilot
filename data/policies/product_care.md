# Product Care Guide

## Linen Products

Linen garments should be washed using a gentle cycle with mild detergent.

Customers should avoid high heat when drying linen products.

## Denim Products

Denim products should normally be washed inside out using cold water.

Frequent high-temperature washing may cause colour loss.

## Returned Products

Products returned under the standard return policy should not show signs of wear, washing or alteration.

Products that have been used beyond normal inspection may not qualify for a standard return.
